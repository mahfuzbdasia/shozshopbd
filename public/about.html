Built around the dial, not the marketing.

Our thesis

A watch should tell you two things: the time, and the truth about what you paid for.

SOHOZ SHOP BD started with a simple frustration: watch listings that lead with lifestyle photography and bury the movement type in the fourth paragraph. We list case size, movement, water resistance and strap material on every product page, above the fold, in the same format every time.

Twenty references across five collections — Classic, Chronograph, Diver, Sport and Minimalist — each built to a clear brief rather than a trend cycle.

How we got here

A short timeline

Year 1

Started with three references

Meridian Steel, Solstice Noir and Aurora Slim — one classic, one dress-black, one minimalist. Sold directly, no distributor markup.

Year 2

Added the dive and chronograph lines

Horizon Dive 300 was pressure-tested independently before listing. Zenith Chrono followed after customer requests for a timing complication.

Year 3

Nationwide delivery and cash on delivery

Partnered with local couriers to reach every division in Bangladesh, with COD as a standard payment option from day one.

Today

Twenty references, one spec sheet format

Every watch on the site lists the same four specs, in the same order, so comparing two references takes seconds.

What we won't do

Three commitments

No inflated "was" prices

Sale prices are only shown against a price the watch actually sold at.

Movement type, always stated

Automatic, quartz or manual is listed on every reference — never left ambiguous.

Real stock counts

If a reference shows low stock, it's because it's actually running low.
